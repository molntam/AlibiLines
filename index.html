    Alibi Lines Case 001 · Timed-evidence prototype
    Detective briefing

    How to play

    Reconstruct six missing timelines.

    Cameras, access logs and physical traces establish where each person was at particular minutes. The route itself must explain every record.

    1. 1

      Select a person. Only that person's evidence pins appear on the floor plan.

    2. 2

      Draw orthogonally. Every move advances the clock by one minute; use Wait to remain in place for a minute.

    3. 3

      Walls stop movement. Change rooms only through a marked door, gate or hatch, and never cross fixed furniture.

    4. 4

      There is no route-length clue. The clock and the timestamps determine how far a valid timeline can travel.

    5. 5

      Different people's routes may cross or overlap. A single timeline cannot return to a tile after leaving it.

    6. 6

      Verify all five suspects and Adrian. The murderer remains sealed until every timeline is complete.

    Final deduction

    Location is not enough. Only a same-tile, same-minute encounter inside the forensic window identifies the murderer.
